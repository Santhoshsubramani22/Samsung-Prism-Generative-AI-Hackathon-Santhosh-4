Company Travel Reimbursement Policy

Domestic Travel
Employees travelling on approved domestic business trips may claim eligible transportation, lodging, and meal expenses according to the limits defined in this policy. Original receipts are required for reimbursable expenses unless a documented exception applies.

International Travel
International business travel requires prior approval from the employee's manager and the travel administration team. International reimbursement claims must include the approved travel request, itemized receipts, and the final travel itinerary.

Transportation
Economy-class airfare is reimbursable for standard business travel. Higher cabin classes require explicit prior authorization.

Meals
Meal reimbursement is subject to the applicable daily allowance. Claims above the applicable allowance require documented approval.

Submission
Travel reimbursement claims should be submitted within 30 calendar days after completion of the trip. The claim should identify the trip dates, destination, business purpose, and supporting receipts.
