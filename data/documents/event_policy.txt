Corporate Event Policy

Venue Capacity
The main event hall supports a maximum of 500 attendees. The training room supports up to 80 attendees.

Catering Options
Approved catering options include vegetarian meals, non-vegetarian meals, snacks, tea, and coffee. Special dietary requests should be submitted with the event request.

Cancellation Policy
Venue cancellation requests should be submitted to the administration team as early as possible. Cancellation charges depend on the timing and the terms of the venue agreement.

Event Approval
Events require an approved request containing the event purpose, expected attendance, venue requirement, and estimated budget.
